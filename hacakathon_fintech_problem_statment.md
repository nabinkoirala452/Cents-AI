# **Our Idea**

## CentsAI

### An Agentic AI Finance Copilot for Small Service Agencies

**Tagline:** Understand your cash. Protect your margins. Run your agency with confidence.

## 1. Target Users

CentsAI is designed first for small service agencies with approximately 2–20 employees, such as:

- Software development and IT consulting firms
- Digital marketing and SEO agencies
- Design, branding, and creative studios
- Business and professional-service consultancies

These agencies earn money through projects, milestones, retainers, and client invoices. At the same time, they must cover regular costs such as salaries, freelancers and contractors, rent, software subscriptions, taxes, and loan repayments. If a client pays late, an agency can struggle to meet payroll or other obligations even when its projects are profitable.

CentsAI is not intended to replace a full accounting, payroll, or project-management system. It brings key financial information together and helps owners understand what it means and what to do next.

## 2. Problem Statement

Small service agencies often manage their finances across spreadsheets, invoices, payment platforms, and separate expense records. Agency owners may not have a dedicated finance team, making it difficult to know which clients have unpaid invoices, whether projects are profitable, how much cash will be available when payroll and contractor payments are due, or whether the agency can afford to hire another employee or take on a new commitment.

The problem is especially challenging when revenue is irregular: a large project invoice may be paid late while salaries, software subscriptions, rent, taxes, and loan instalments still need to be paid on time. Basic dashboards report historical figures, but owners also need to understand future cash shortages and take appropriate action.

CentsAI aims to address this gap through an agentic AI finance copilot built specifically for small service agencies. It consolidates agency income and expenses, tracks client invoices and retainers, monitors payroll and contractor obligations, records loans and EMIs, estimates project and client profitability, and forecasts future cash flow. It explains financial risks in plain language and recommends practical next steps, such as following up on an overdue client invoice, reviewing a rising software expense, or delaying a discretionary purchase.

CentsAI will integrate supported PayPal payment workflows so an agency can create and manage eligible client payment requests and verify their status. For the hackathon prototype, payment operations will use PayPal Sandbox accounts, with explicit user approval, backend verification, and an audit trail. The AI will prepare and explain actions; the application’s deterministic business logic and permission checks will control calculations and execution.

## 3. How CentsAI Fits Both Hackathons

### FinTechathon — Topic E: SME Finance Copilot

CentsAI supports small service agencies with cash-flow management, financial analysis, financing insights, alerts, and decision support. Its agency-specific features include project profitability, client payment tracking, contractor costs, payroll obligations, recurring software expenses, and loan repayment planning.

### PayPal AI Hackathon

CentsAI meaningfully integrates PayPal’s developer platform into an AI-assisted payment workflow. The prototype will demonstrate supported invoice or payment-request operations in Sandbox, payment status verification, and financial-record updates after a verified result. The integration is central to the workflow rather than a decorative checkout button.

## 4. Core Features

### 1. Agency Finance Dashboard

Provide one view of the agency’s financial position:

- Current recorded cash balance and recent money in and money out
- Client invoices: paid, unpaid, overdue, and due soon
- Upcoming salaries, contractor payments, subscriptions, taxes, rent, and EMIs
- Monthly revenue, operating costs, and estimated net cash flow
- Retainer income and project milestone payments expected
- Alerts for issues needing the owner’s attention

### 2. Income and Expense Management

Track income and expenses by category, client, project, and month.

- Record client payments, project revenue, retainers, and other income
- Track salaries, freelance and contractor costs, rent, utilities, software tools, marketing, travel, tax obligations, and other operating costs
- Import transactions from a CSV file or enter them manually for the prototype
- Categorize expenses and identify recurring subscriptions
- Flag possible duplicate entries and unusual changes for review
- Calculate total money in, total money out, and net cash flow

### 3. Client Invoice and Payment Collection Agent

Help agencies get paid for completed work.

- Create and track client invoices, due dates, milestones, and retainers
- Identify overdue and soon-to-be-due invoices
- Prioritize follow-ups by overdue duration, amount, and expected cash-flow impact
- Draft professional, personalized payment reminders
- Use supported PayPal APIs to create and manage eligible invoices or payment requests
- Verify payment status through the backend and update the agency ledger only after verification
- Keep a record of reminders, approvals, and payment events

**Example:** “Two client invoices totaling ₹85,000 are overdue. If neither is paid before payroll, your forecast shows a possible ₹25,000 shortfall. Would you like to review reminder drafts?”

### 4. Agency Cash-Flow Forecasting and Early Warnings

Estimate available cash over the next 7, 30, and 90 days using current recorded balances, expected client receipts, retainer renewals, payroll, contractor costs, subscriptions, tax obligations, and loan instalments.

- Show expected, optimistic, and conservative scenarios
- Separate confirmed cash and payments from forecast or uncertain receipts
- Flag dates when the projected balance may fall below a threshold
- Explain which client payments or costs contribute to each warning
- Recommend practical responses, such as following up on invoices or revisiting discretionary expenses
- Update projections when a payment is verified or an assumption changes

### 5. Payroll and Contractor Cost Planning

Give the owner visibility into people-related financial commitments.

- Maintain salary amounts and expected pay dates
- Track contractor invoices, agreed amounts, and payment status
- Calculate total expected payroll and contractor obligations for a period
- Estimate the effect of a new hire, salary increase, or additional contractor on cash flow
- Prepare payment records and reminders
- Support payout execution only if the relevant payment product, account access, and eligibility are confirmed; otherwise, track the obligation without claiming it has been paid

### 6. Loan, Interest, and EMI Intelligence

Help agencies understand existing debt and compare financing offers.

- Record loan principal, interest rate, term, EMI, due dates, and outstanding balance
- Calculate scheduled instalments and estimated remaining principal and interest using a defined repayment method
- Compare owner-provided loan offers by interest, fees, tenure, EMI, and total borrowing cost
- Simulate additional repayments and estimate possible interest savings
- Flag repayment dates that may coincide with cash-flow shortages
- Explain assumptions, fees, and any prepayment charges entered by the user

### 7. Project and Client Profitability Intelligence

This is a key feature specific to service agencies. Revenue alone does not show whether a client or project is profitable.

- Track project fees, milestone payments, and retainer revenue
- Record or import estimated hours, billable hours, and non-billable hours where available
- Attribute employee and contractor costs to a project using owner-provided rates or cost assumptions
- Estimate gross project margin and compare budgeted costs with recorded costs
- Identify projects with rising costs, repeated rework, or scope changes when the data is available
- Compare profitability across clients and projects

**Example:** “Project Orion has generated ₹1,50,000 in fees, but recorded employee and contractor costs are ₹1,18,000. Its estimated contribution before shared overhead is ₹32,000. Review the assumptions and unrecorded costs before making a decision.”

### 8. Expense Detective and Subscription Review

Help agencies control recurring overheads.

- Group expenses by tools and services, such as hosting, design software, collaboration tools, cloud services, and marketing platforms
- Compare monthly spending and flag unusual increases
- Identify recurring subscriptions for the owner to review
- Spot duplicate-looking expenses for human verification
- Suggest possible cost-saving opportunities without automatically cancelling services

**Example:** “Your software subscriptions increased by 18% this month, mainly due to two new tools. Review whether both are still needed.”

### 9. “Can We Afford This?” Agency Simulator

Let owners test business decisions before committing money.

- Simulate hiring an employee or contractor
- Evaluate a new software subscription or equipment purchase
- Model a client paying an invoice late
- Estimate the effect of losing a retainer or experiencing a revenue decline
- Compare delaying a discretionary expense with proceeding now
- Explain the assumptions and predicted impact on cash availability

**Example:** “Can we hire another developer at ₹45,000 per month if one client invoice may be delayed by 30 days?”

### 10. Business Financing Assistant

Help an agency estimate its funding needs and understand the trade-offs between financing and other options.

- Estimate a potential funding shortfall from the cash-flow forecast
- Compare financing offers supplied by the owner
- Calculate estimated repayments and total borrowing costs
- Assess affordability against revenue, expenses, existing debt, and projected cash flow
- Provide a loan-readiness checklist and list records the agency may need
- Compare borrowing with alternative actions, such as collecting overdue invoices or postponing discretionary costs

CentsAI will not promise loan approval or claim that an external lender will accept the agency.

### 11. Daily AI Finance Briefing

Provide a short summary of what the owner needs to know each day.

- Current recorded cash position and recent changes
- Client invoices overdue or due soon
- Payroll, contractor payments, taxes, subscriptions, and EMIs coming up
- Project or client margin changes that need attention
- Potential cash-flow warnings and the reasons behind them
- Suggested priorities for the day

### 12. Conversational AI Finance Copilot

Allow owners to ask questions in natural language, with answers grounded in the agency’s recorded financial data and calculation results.

**Example questions:**

- “How much money came in and went out this month?”
- “Which clients have overdue invoices?”
- “Can we cover payroll if Client A pays two weeks late?”
- “Which projects have the lowest estimated margins?”
- “How much do we owe in salaries, contractor invoices, and EMIs this month?”
- “Can we afford to hire another developer?”

The AI should cite or display the underlying records and assumptions where useful, identify missing data, and avoid inventing numbers.

### 13. Approval, Security, and Audit Center

Protect agency financial information and prevent unauthorized actions.

- Separate read-only analysis from payment-execution permissions
- Require an authorized user to review and approve sensitive payment actions
- Show recipient, amount, currency, and relevant fees before confirmation
- Enforce configurable limits and validate payment details
- Keep API secrets on the backend
- Verify payment results and handle pending, failed, or duplicate attempts safely
- Log recommendations, approvals, API requests, and verified outcomes
- Document known limitations, risks, and safeguards in the security self-assessment

## 5. The Core Differentiator: Agency-Aware Financial Agent

CentsAI should connect client billing, project profitability, people costs, and future cash flow rather than treating them as isolated tools.

- **Understand:** Read the agency’s entered or imported financial records, invoices, projects, retainers, costs, and obligations.
- **Analyze:** Calculate revenue, expenses, estimated project margins, debt repayments, and upcoming commitments using deterministic logic.
- **Predict:** Forecast cash shortages and show how overdue invoices or new commitments may affect the agency.
- **Explain:** Describe the cause of a financial risk in language a non-finance founder can understand.
- **Recommend:** Suggest concrete options such as following up on an overdue invoice, reviewing a subscription, or postponing a discretionary expense.
- **Act safely:** Prepare supported PayPal invoice or payment operations and request required approval.
- **Verify:** Confirm the API result, update records only when supported by verified status, and keep an audit trail.

## 6. Example End-to-End Workflow

A software agency has eight employees. It expects ₹3,00,000 from monthly retainers and project milestones, but a ₹1,00,000 client invoice is overdue. Payroll of ₹1,80,000 and other bills of ₹70,000 are due soon.

CentsAI identifies the possible cash shortfall, shows which obligations are due first, and explains how much depends on the overdue invoice. It drafts a reminder for the owner to review, supports creation of an eligible PayPal invoice or payment request in Sandbox, and verifies the payment status through the backend. When the payment is confirmed, the ledger and cash-flow forecast are updated. If payment remains pending, the forecast continues to treat the receipt as uncertain.

This workflow shows how the financial analysis, AI recommendation, PayPal integration, owner approval, and outcome verification work together.
